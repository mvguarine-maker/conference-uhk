THE MEETING

About the meeting

The Czech–Polish Meeting on Graded Geometry brings together researchers interested in graded geometry and related areas of mathematics. The meeting offers an opportunity to present current work, exchange ideas, and discuss questions across these fields.

It will take place in Hradec Králové, Czech Republic, from 19 to 23 October 2026.
